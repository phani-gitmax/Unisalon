Hyderabad is tough on hair. Months of strong sun, dust on busy roads like the ones around Manikonda and Gachibowli, and hard borewell water all strip moisture from your hair and scalp. The result is something we hear about every day at our Alkapur Township branches: **dryness, frizz, dullness and more hair fall than usual**.

A professional hair spa is one of the simplest ways to fight back. Here's what it actually does.

## What happens during a hair spa?

A hair spa at Unisalon has four steps:

1. **Cleanse** — a gentle shampoo suited to your scalp type removes dust, oil and product build-up.
2. **Treat** — a nourishing L'Oréal mask (with an optional concentrate for extra repair) is worked through the lengths.
3. **Massage and steam** — a relaxing head massage boosts circulation while steam helps the treatment absorb.
4. **Rinse and style** — your hair is rinsed, conditioned and blow-dried.

The whole session takes about 45–60 minutes.

## 6 benefits of a hair spa

### 1. Deep moisture for sun-dried hair
UV exposure breaks down the outer layer of the hair. Spa masks replace lost moisture and lipids, so hair feels softer straight away.

### 2. Less frizz
Frizz is mostly dry hair pulling moisture from the air. Once hair is properly hydrated, it lies smoother — especially helpful in humid monsoon months.

### 3. A cleaner, healthier scalp
Dust and sweat clog the scalp. A thorough cleanse and massage removes build-up and leaves the scalp feeling fresh.

### 4. Helps with hard-water damage
Hard water leaves mineral deposits that make hair rough and dull. Regular spas help restore softness and shine.

### 5. Repair after colour, ironing or keratin
Chemical treatments and daily heat styling weaken hair. A spa with concentrate is designed for this kind of damage.

### 6. Genuine relaxation
The head massage is the part most clients look forward to — 15 minutes of calm in a busy week.

## How often should you get a hair spa?

- **Dry, coloured or treated hair:** every 3–4 weeks
- **Normal hair:** once a month or before special occasions
- **Men with short hair:** every 4–6 weeks, mainly for scalp health
