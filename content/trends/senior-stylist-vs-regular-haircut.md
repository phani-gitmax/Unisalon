## What's the difference?

Every Unisalon stylist is trained to give a clean, accurate cut. Senior stylists have **many more years behind the chair** and are the people we trust with:

- **Big changes** — going from long to short, a new fringe or layers for the first time
- **Precision styles** — sharp fades, textured crops, bobs and lobs
- **Problem hair** — cowlicks, very thick or very fine hair, uneven regrowth
- **Fixing a bad cut** from somewhere else

## Choose a regular haircut if…

- You're keeping your current style and just need a trim
- You know exactly what you want and get it cut every few weeks
- It's a quick tidy-up before an event

## Choose a senior stylist if…

- You want a new look and need advice on what suits your face shape
- You're preparing for a wedding, interview or photoshoot
- Your hair is hard to manage and you want a cut that grows out well

## Tips for getting the haircut you want

1. **Bring a photo** — it's the easiest way to explain a style.
2. **Mention your routine** — do you blow-dry every day or leave it natural?
3. **Talk about length in centimetres or inches**, not "a little".
4. **Book ahead on weekends** — Saturdays and Sundays are our busiest days.
